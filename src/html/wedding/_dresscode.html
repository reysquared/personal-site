Dress Code

Many people have been asking us about this. Of the events, only the ceremony + reception have a dress code, the short version of which is, don't worry too much and get freaky wit it! The long version follows.

Choose ONE OR MORE of the following subclasses:

The Comfy

Wear whatever makes you feel most comfortable — no starched collars or tight zippers here. If they're calling to you, you can literally wear your pajamas. Be free.

The Cunty

Wear any outfit that makes you feel Fly. As. Hell. Get audacious with it. Come dressed to turn heads and slay the house down. Cover girl, give a twirl!

The Iconoclast

Wear something you would never wear to any other wedding. No holds barred — with the caveats that 1) my baby cousins will be in attendance and 2) the venue for the reception is largely outdoors.

We are earnest about this!!! If your idea of "whatever makes you most comfortable" is honestly just traditional wedding semi-formal, that's okay too... just know it's not entirely in the spirit Bring your sense of whimsy, and if you're bringing any weeuns, please encourage them to TRULY get as weird as they want to with the 'fit as well!
